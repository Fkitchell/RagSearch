The Fox and the Crow

A crow found a piece of cheese and flew up to a high branch to enjoy it. A fox passing below caught the smell and wanted the cheese for himself. "Good morning, beautiful bird," he called. "What glossy feathers you have, and what bright eyes! Surely a creature so lovely must have a voice to match. If only I could hear you sing, I would know you are the queen of all birds."

The crow, flattered beyond all sense, opened her beak to give her finest caw. The cheese dropped straight down, and the fox snapped it up. "Thank you," he said. "You have a voice, it seems. What you lack is wits."

Moral: Beware of those who flatter you.
