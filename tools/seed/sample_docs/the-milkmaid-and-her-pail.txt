The Milkmaid and Her Pail

A milkmaid walked to market with a pail of fresh milk balanced on her head. As she went, she daydreamed. "With the money from this milk I will buy some eggs. The eggs will hatch into chickens. I will sell the chickens and buy a new dress, and when I wear it to the fair all the young men will want to dance with me. But I will toss my head and pass them by."

And she tossed her head. The pail tumbled off, and all the milk spilled into the dust. The eggs, the chickens, the dress, and the dancing vanished with it.

Moral: Do not count your chickens before they are hatched.
