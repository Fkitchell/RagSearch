The Stag at the Pool

A stag came to a clear pool to drink and, gazing at his reflection, admired his great branching antlers. "What a noble crown I wear," he thought. Then he looked at his legs and frowned. "But how thin and ugly these are. I am ashamed of them."

Just then a pack of hounds burst from the trees. The stag bounded away across the open plain, and his slender legs carried him far ahead of the dogs. But when he reached the woods, his antlers caught in the low branches and held him fast. As the hounds closed in, he cried, "The legs I despised would have saved me, and the antlers I was so proud of have destroyed me."

Moral: We often value what is showy over what is truly useful.
