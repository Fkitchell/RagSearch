The Ant and the Grasshopper

All summer long a grasshopper hopped about the fields, chirping and singing in the warm sun. Nearby an ant worked from dawn to dusk, hauling grains of wheat and corn back to her nest one by one. "Why not come and sing with me?" the grasshopper called. "There is food everywhere. Why wear yourself out?" The ant replied that she was storing food for the winter and suggested the grasshopper do the same. The grasshopper only laughed and went on singing.

When winter came the fields were bare and buried in snow. The grasshopper, weak with hunger, knocked at the ant's door and begged for a few grains. "What were you doing all summer while I was working?" asked the ant. "I was singing," said the grasshopper. "Then," said the ant, "you may dance all winter."

Moral: Prepare today for the needs of tomorrow.
