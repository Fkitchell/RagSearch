The Wolf and the Lamb

A wolf drinking at a stream saw a lamb drinking a little way downstream and decided to make a meal of it, but he wanted some excuse first. "How dare you muddy the water I am drinking," he growled. "I could not have, sir," said the lamb. "The water flows from you to me." "Well then," said the wolf, "last year you insulted me." "That cannot be," said the lamb. "I was not born a year ago." "Then it was your mother," snarled the wolf, "and it makes no difference." And he seized the lamb and ate it.

Moral: A tyrant will always find a pretext for his cruelty.
