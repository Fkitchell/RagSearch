The Bundle of Sticks

An old farmer had several sons who were always quarreling. Nothing he said would make them stop. One day he called them together and handed them a bundle of sticks tied tightly with a cord. "Break it," he said. Each son in turn tried with all his strength, but the bundle would not break.

Then the father untied the cord and gave each son a single stick. They snapped them easily. "You see," said the father. "As long as you stand together, no one can harm you. But if you quarrel and go your separate ways, you will be broken one at a time, as easily as these sticks."

Moral: In unity there is strength.
