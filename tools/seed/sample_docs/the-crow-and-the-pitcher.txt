The Crow and the Pitcher

A crow, half dead with thirst after a long dry spell, came upon a pitcher that had once held water. He pushed his beak inside, but only a little water remained at the bottom, and he could not reach it. He tried tipping the pitcher over, but it was too heavy. Just as he was about to give up, an idea came to him.

He picked up a pebble and dropped it into the pitcher. Then another, and another. With each pebble the water rose a little higher. At last it came within reach, and the crow drank until his thirst was gone.

Moral: Necessity is the mother of invention.
