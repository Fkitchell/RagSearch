The Crab and Its Mother

A mother crab watched her son scuttling sideways across the wet sand. "My child," she said, "you should walk straight ahead. It is much more graceful." "I would gladly, Mother," replied the young crab, "if you will show me how." The mother tried to walk forward and went sideways just as he did. She tried again, and again went sideways, and finally said nothing more about it.

Moral: Example teaches better than advice.
