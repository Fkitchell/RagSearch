The Wolf in Sheep's Clothing

A wolf found it hard to get near the flock because the shepherd and his dogs watched so carefully. One day he came upon the skin of a sheep that had been flayed and thrown aside. He pulled it over his back and slipped in among the sheep as they grazed. The lambs followed him without fear, and at nightfall the shepherd shut him into the fold with the rest.

That evening, however, the shepherd wanted mutton for supper. He went into the fold, picked out what looked like the plumpest sheep, and killed it on the spot. It was the wolf.

Moral: Appearances can deceive, and the deceiver may be caught in his own trap.
