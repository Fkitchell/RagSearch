The Dog in the Manger

A dog looking for a comfortable place to nap jumped into a manger full of hay and curled up. Later the ox came in from the field, tired and hungry, and went to eat. The dog jumped up and snarled and snapped at him, refusing to let him near the hay. The ox tried again and again, but each time the dog bared his teeth. At last the ox gave up and walked away. "What a mean creature," he muttered. "He cannot eat the hay himself, yet he will not let anyone else have it."

Moral: Do not begrudge others what you cannot use yourself.
