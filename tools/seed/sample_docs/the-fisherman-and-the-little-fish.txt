The Fisherman and the Little Fish

A fisherman who had caught nothing all day finally pulled up his line and found a tiny fish wriggling on the hook. "Please let me go," begged the fish. "I am far too small to be worth eating. Throw me back, and when I am grown big and fat you can catch me again and have a fine meal." The fisherman shook his head and dropped it into his basket. "I would be a fool," he said, "to give up a fish in my hand for the hope of a bigger one someday."

Moral: A small gain that is certain is worth more than a large one that is only promised.
