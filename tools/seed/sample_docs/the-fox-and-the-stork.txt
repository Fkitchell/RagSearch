The Fox and the Stork

A fox invited a stork to dinner, meaning to have some fun at her expense. He served soup in a wide, flat dish. The fox lapped it up easily, but the stork, with her long thin beak, could only wet the tip of it and went home as hungry as she came. "I am sorry the soup was not to your taste," the fox said, hiding a grin.

A few days later the stork invited the fox to dinner in return. She served a fragrant stew in a tall jar with a narrow neck. The stork reached in with her beak and ate her fill, while the fox could only lick the outside of the jar and sniff the smell. "I will not apologize for the dinner," said the stork. "One bad turn deserves another."

Moral: Those who play tricks should expect tricks in return.
