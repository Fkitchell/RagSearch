Belling the Cat

The mice held a meeting to decide what to do about the cat, who had been catching them one after another. Many plans were offered and rejected. At last a young mouse stood up. "I have it," he said. "We will hang a bell around the cat's neck. Then whenever she comes near, we will hear her and have time to escape." The mice cheered and clapped. It seemed a perfect plan.

Then an old mouse who had said nothing so far rose slowly to his feet. "It is a fine idea," he said. "Only one question remains. Which of us will bell the cat?" The mice looked at one another, and no one said a word.

Moral: It is one thing to propose a plan and another to carry it out.
