The Farmer and the Viper

On a bitter winter morning a farmer found a viper lying stiff and frozen on the path. Feeling sorry for it, he picked it up and placed it inside his coat to warm it. Slowly the snake came back to life. As soon as it had recovered its strength, it turned and bit the man who had saved it. Dying, the farmer said, "I have only myself to blame for showing pity to a creature whose nature I knew."

Moral: Kindness is wasted on those who are wicked by nature.
