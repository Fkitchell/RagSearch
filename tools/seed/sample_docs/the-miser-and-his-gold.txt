The Miser and His Gold

A miser sold everything he owned and bought a lump of gold, which he buried in a hole at the foot of an old wall. Every day he went to dig it up, look at it, and bury it again. A workman who noticed his visits grew curious, and one night he dug up the gold and ran off with it.

The next morning the miser found the hole empty and began to howl and tear his hair. A neighbor came to see what was wrong. When he heard the story, he said, "Do not grieve so much. Put a stone in the hole and come look at it every day. You never used the gold anyway, so the stone will do you just as much good."

Moral: Wealth that is never used is worth no more than a stone.
