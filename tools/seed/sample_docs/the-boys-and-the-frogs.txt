The Boys and the Frogs

Some boys were playing at the edge of a pond where a family of frogs lived. For fun they began throwing stones into the water, cheering whenever one splashed near a frog. Several frogs were struck and badly hurt. At last the oldest frog lifted his head above the water and called out, "Please stop, boys. What is sport to you is death to us."

Moral: Think about how your amusement affects others.
