The Man, the Boy, and the Donkey

A man and his son were taking their donkey to market. As they walked beside it, a passerby laughed. "What fools, walking when they have a donkey to ride." So the man put the boy on the donkey. Soon they passed some old men who grumbled, "Look at that lazy boy riding while his poor father walks." So the man told the boy to get down and climbed on himself.

A little farther on, some women called out, "Shame on that man for riding while his little son trudges along." So the man pulled the boy up beside him. Then a townsman scolded them. "Two of you on one small donkey? You will break its back. You would do better to carry it." So the man and the boy tied the donkey's legs to a pole and carried it on their shoulders.

As they crossed a bridge into town, the crowd roared with laughter. The frightened donkey kicked free, fell into the river, and was swept away. The man walked home with nothing.

Moral: Try to please everyone and you will please no one.
