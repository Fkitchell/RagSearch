The Dog and His Reflection

A dog had stolen a juicy bone from a butcher's shop and was carrying it home in his mouth. On the way he crossed a narrow plank over a still stream. Looking down, he saw another dog in the water holding a bone that seemed even bigger than his own. Greedy for both, he snapped at the dog in the water. As he opened his jaws, his own bone fell into the stream and sank out of sight.

Moral: Grasp at the shadow and you may lose the substance.
