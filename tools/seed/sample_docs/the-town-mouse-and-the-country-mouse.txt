The Town Mouse and the Country Mouse

A country mouse invited his cousin from the town to visit. He served a simple meal of barley, roots, and a few acorns, all he had. The town mouse nibbled politely, then said, "Cousin, how can you live on such plain food? Come home with me and I will show you how to live."

So the two set off for the town. In a grand house they found the remains of a feast: cakes, cheese, jellies, and fruit. The country mouse had never seen such things. But they had barely begun to eat when the door burst open and servants came in, and the mice had to scramble into a crack in the wall. When it was quiet they crept out again, only to be chased off by a pair of barking dogs.

"Goodbye, cousin," said the country mouse, gathering himself. "You may keep your fine food. I would rather eat my barley in peace than feast in fear."

Moral: A simple life with peace is better than luxury with worry.
