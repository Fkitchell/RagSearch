The Frogs Who Desired a King

The frogs of a quiet pond grew bored with their freedom and begged the great god Jupiter to send them a king. Jupiter, amused, dropped a large log into the pond. It landed with such a splash that the frogs dove to the bottom in terror. After a while they peeked out, saw the log lying still, and one by one they swam up to it, climbed on it, and finally sat on it without fear. Soon they were complaining that their king was useless and dull.

They begged Jupiter for a real king, one who would rule them. Annoyed, Jupiter sent them a stork. The stork strode into the pond and began gobbling up frogs one after another. Now the frogs pleaded to have their old peace back, but Jupiter would not listen. "You wanted a king," he said, "and now you have one."

Moral: Be careful what you wish for.
