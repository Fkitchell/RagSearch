The Eagle and the Arrow

An eagle sat high on a rocky crag, watching for a hare in the valley below. A hunter hidden among the rocks saw her, took careful aim, and let fly an arrow. It struck the eagle in the breast. As she fell, she looked at the shaft and saw that it was fletched with her own feathers, shed long ago and picked up by the hunter. "How much sharper the wound," she cried, "when it comes from something of my own."

Moral: We often give our enemies the means of our own destruction.
