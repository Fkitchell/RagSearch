The Goose That Laid the Golden Eggs

A farmer and his wife owned a goose that, to their astonishment, laid a single egg of solid gold every morning. They sold the eggs and slowly grew rich. But the richer they became, the more impatient they grew. "If the goose lays gold," the farmer said one night, "she must be full of gold inside. Why wait for one egg a day when we could have it all at once?"

So they killed the goose and cut her open. Inside she was exactly like any other goose. There was no gold at all, and now there would be no more eggs.

Moral: Greed often destroys the very thing that feeds it.
