The Tortoise and the Hare

A hare was forever boasting about how fast he could run, and he loved to tease the tortoise for her slow, plodding steps. One day the tortoise grew tired of it. "Let us race," she said, "and we will see who reaches the old oak first." The hare laughed so hard he nearly fell over, but he agreed, and the animals of the meadow gathered to watch.

At the signal the hare shot away and was soon out of sight. Halfway to the oak he looked back and saw no sign of the tortoise. Feeling very pleased with himself, he lay down in the shade for a short nap. The tortoise, meanwhile, kept going one steady step after another. She passed the sleeping hare without a sound.

When the hare woke, the sun was low. He dashed toward the oak as fast as his legs would carry him, only to find the tortoise already resting beneath it while the crowd cheered.

Moral: Slow and steady wins the race.
