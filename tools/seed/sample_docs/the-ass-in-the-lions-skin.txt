The Ass in the Lion's Skin

An ass found a lion's skin left behind by hunters and pulled it over himself. Delighted with his disguise, he strolled about the countryside, and every animal that saw him fled in terror. Puffed up with his new importance, he let out a loud bray to frighten them even more. A fox who heard it laughed out loud. "I might have been afraid of you too," he said, "if I had not heard your voice."

Moral: Fine clothes may disguise a fool, but his words will reveal him.
