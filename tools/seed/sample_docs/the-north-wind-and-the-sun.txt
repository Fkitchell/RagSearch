The North Wind and the Sun

The North Wind and the Sun argued over which of them was stronger. Seeing a traveler on the road below, they agreed that whoever could make him take off his cloak would be the winner.

The North Wind went first. He blew a cold, howling blast that bent the trees and stung the traveler's face. But the harder he blew, the more tightly the man wrapped his cloak around himself. At last the Wind gave up.

Then the Sun came out from behind a cloud and shone down gently. The traveler loosened his cloak. The Sun grew warmer and warmer, until the man, sweating, pulled off his cloak and sat down to rest in the shade of a tree.

Moral: Gentle persuasion often succeeds where force fails.
