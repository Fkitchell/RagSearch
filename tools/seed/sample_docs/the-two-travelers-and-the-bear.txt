The Two Travelers and the Bear

Two friends were traveling together through a forest when a bear suddenly crashed out of the bushes ahead of them. One of the men, quick and nimble, scrambled up a tree and hid among the branches without a thought for his companion. The other, seeing he could not escape, threw himself flat on the ground and held his breath, for he had heard that bears will not touch a dead body.

The bear came up and sniffed all around him, putting its muzzle close to his ear. The man lay perfectly still. At last the bear lost interest and lumbered away. When it was gone, the man in the tree climbed down and asked, with a laugh, what the bear had whispered in his ear. "It gave me some good advice," his friend replied. "It said never to travel with a friend who deserts you at the first sign of danger."

Moral: Misfortune tests the sincerity of friends.
