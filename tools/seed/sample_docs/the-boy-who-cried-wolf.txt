The Boy Who Cried Wolf

A shepherd boy was set to watch the village sheep on a hillside above the town. The days were long and dull, and to amuse himself he ran down the slope one morning shouting, "Wolf! Wolf! A wolf is after the sheep!" The villagers dropped their work and rushed up the hill with sticks and pitchforks, only to find the boy laughing and the flock grazing peacefully.

A week later he played the same trick, and again the villagers came running, and again he laughed at them. They went home grumbling.

Then one evening a real wolf crept out of the forest and began scattering the flock. The boy ran toward the village crying, "Wolf! Wolf! Please, this time it is true!" But the villagers only shook their heads and went on with their supper, certain he was fooling them again. The wolf had its fill, and many sheep were lost.

Moral: A liar will not be believed, even when he tells the truth.
